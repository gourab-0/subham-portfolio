About Me

Crafting visuals that
mean something.

Mumbai-based Graphic Designer and VFX Artist with a passion for bold, purposeful visual storytelling. With 2+ years of hands-on VFX training and a business-centric mindset shaped by a B.Com degree, I craft work that doesn't just look good — it communicates. My portfolio spans self-initiated concept projects — like the Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra campaign visuals and magazine editorial layouts — as well as real client work, most notably the complete Tea Company Cafe brand identity, delivered end-to-end: logo, menus, launch campaigns, and live storefront. Every project, personal or professional, is built with intent, precision, and a deep respect for the audience it speaks to.
